🎯 Your mission

Put the numbers in order from 1 to 8. The empty space belongs at the bottom-right.

Ready? Tap a tile next to the empty space to slide it.

Tip: A tile can move only when it touches the empty space. You can also drag the board to look around.

How to play

Tap or click a tile beside the empty slot. Arrange the tiles as 1, 2, 3 / 4, 5, 6 / 7, 8, empty. Press New puzzle for a fresh solvable challenge. This demo runs in your browser; it does not need an account.
